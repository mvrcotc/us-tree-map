**Subject:** Permission to publish hex summaries derived from Tree Equity Score (free, noncommercial map)

Hello,

I'm building the US Tree Map, a free, public-good web map that helps people see how tree cover has changed and screen which native trees fit a place, now and under future climate. It's open source (MIT) with no ads or fees. The pilot covers Wisconsin.

I'd like to use Tree Equity Score as the "planting need" layer. Specifically:

- **What we'd publish:** area-weighted summaries on a hexagon grid (H3, cells of about 0.7 to 36 km²): the average score, average canopy share, and the share of each hexagon covered by scored neighborhoods. We wouldn't redistribute your block-group data or boundaries.
- **How it's credited:** "Tree Equity Score, American Forests" with a link, on the layer, in its legend and tooltips, and in the app's About panel, plus any citation you prefer.
- **How it's used:** need is one of four factors behind a screening list of species, always shown next to its reasons. The app says it is a screening tool, not planting advice.

Your site doesn't state a license for the data, so I'd rather ask than assume. Could you confirm in writing that we may publish these derived values, and tell me any terms or attribution wording you'd like? I'm happy to share a preview before launch.

Thank you for making Tree Equity Score available.

Best regards,
Marco Torres
US Tree Map · https://github.com/mvrcotc/us-tree-map
